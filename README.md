# Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Overview
This project is part of my Data Analyst Internship at Karmix Tech. It provides 
a high-level, company-wide view of Adidas US sales performance, covering revenue, 
profit, retailers, regions, and sales channels.

## Business Problem
Provide an executive-level snapshot of business performance — identifying top 
performing retailers, regions, and sales channels to guide strategic decisions.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX

## Key Insights
- West Gear is the top-performing retailer, followed by Foot Locker and Sports Direct
- West region leads in sales, with Midwest trailing behind
- Outlet is the strongest sales channel (39.63% of sales), followed by Online (32.85%)
- Overall: $899.90M total sales, $332.13M operating profit, 42.30% average operating margin

## Recommendation
Analyze what drives West Gear and West region's strong performance and apply 
similar strategies to underperforming regions like Midwest. Evaluate whether 
outlet-driven volume is protecting or eroding overall margin.

## Dataset
Adidas US Sales Dataset (Kaggle)
